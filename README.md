## Ohlone College's first-ever Formula SAE team. Renegades designing, fabricating, and racing a full open-wheel prototype car from **scratch**.
We are Ohlone College's inaugural Formula SAE program — a team of Renegades building something most community colleges never attempt.

*Formula SAE* is a global collegiate design competition organized by SAE International. Teams design, build, and race a small open-wheel race car from scratch, then present it to panels of professional engineers from automotive and aerospace firms including **GM, Ford, Honda, and Lockheed Martin**.

Being a community college team is not a disadvantage — <u>it is our identity</u>. Our students are working engineers in training, many the first in their families to pursue a technical degree. We bring focus, resourcefulness, and a chip on our shoulder.

We are competing in FSAE Michigan/Lincoln — the largest engineering design competition in the world — alongside 420+ teams from 52 countries. Ohlone Renegades belong on that grid.
